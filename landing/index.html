How it works

Capture

Take photos or a short video of a room. Each capture becomes a 3D point cloud placed next to your floor plan.

Tap

Tap any object. AI names it, estimates its size and value, and gives practical tips about it.

Sort

Move things between rooms, group them by category or plan, and see your whole home on a floor map.

Built for moving, decluttering and knowing what you have

Inventory in minutes

"Find all objects" tags a whole photo at once, with sizes in metres and rough values in euros.

Keep, sell, donate, repair

Mark a plan for each item and line everything up by plan to see what goes where.

Ask about your space

"What could I sell to raise €1,000?" "Will the wardrobe fit in the bedroom?" Answers come from your own inventory.

Your data, your server

Aureality can run on your own NAS. Photos stay at home unless you ask the AI about them.

Early prototype, open to try

Aureality is in its first version. Real depth from the phone's camera, automatic 3D scans and sync across devices are next on the roadmap.
