.are, an open format for reality

Every object Aureality knows about is stored as an .are file (Aureality Reality Entity). It is one open, scale-free format that can describe anything, from a single atom to the whole universe, and that you can keep private or share.

Identity

A stable ID, a scale index and a privacy flag for every entity.

Space and physics

Geometry, materials and composition at any scale.

Relations

What something is made of, and where it lives.

Meaning

Names, notes, memories and AI descriptions.

Timeline

A history of how an object moved and changed.

Ownership

Local encryption by default, optional public proof when you choose to share.
